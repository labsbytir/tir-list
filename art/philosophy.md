# Verdant Drift

An algorithmic philosophy for tir-list's generative artwork: spore dispersal through a forest canopy, rendered as a seeded flow field in brand greens.

## Philosophy

**Computational processes and mathematical relationships.** Verdant Drift treats the catalog as an ecosystem: every listing is a spore released into a canopy of invisible currents. Particles advect through a layered Perlin-noise vector field, their headings recomputed each frame from field angle plus a whisper of curl. Position integrates velocity; velocity integrates the field. Nothing is placed — everything arrives. The composition is the equilibrium state of thousands of simultaneous descents, a meticulously crafted algorithm refined until the drift feels inevitable rather than random, the product of deep computational expertise in flow-field dynamics.

**Noise functions and randomness patterns.** Two noise regimes govern the piece. A low-frequency field sets the prevailing wind — broad, slow currents that carve the composition's large gestures. A high-frequency octave adds local turbulence so no two neighboring spores share a fate. Both octaves draw from a single seeded stream, so the entire weather system is reproducible from one integer. Seeded variation is the point: each seed is a different season in the same forest, and the seed navigator is a time machine across them. This discipline — one seed, total determinism — is the product of painstaking optimization, not convenience.

**Particle behaviors and field dynamics.** Spores spawn at the canvas edges and die at rest or at bounds, then are reborn — dispersal as a steady-state process, mirroring how listings enter and leave a living catalog. Speed is the only biography a spore carries: slow drifters settle into evergreen shadow, mid-tempo travelers glow sea-green, and the fastest burn malachite. A rare few strike lime, the exact accent of the tir-list wordmark's pixel bricks — the conceptual seed hidden in the algorithm, a quiet signature only the observant will catch. Trail persistence smears each journey into ghost-roads, so the final image is a density map of where the forest has been.

**Temporal evolution and system states.** The piece never depicts a moment; it depicts accumulation. Each frame paints the background at near-zero alpha instead of clearing it, so history compounds and the canopy deepens where traffic was dense. The system passes through three perceptible states — ignition as spores first catch the wind, braiding as trails entangle into rivers, and settlement as the density map saturates toward equilibrium. Parameters tune the climate between these states rather than scripting them, and the algorithm was refined through countless iterations until every climate felt intentional.

**Parametric variation and emergent complexity.** Five controls shape the weather: spore count sets the population pressure, flow scale stretches or compresses the wind's wavelength, flow speed throttles every current at once, trail persistence decides how long the forest remembers, and spark probability seeds the rare lime strikes. Three color wells hold the palette but default to the brand's own greens. Nothing here selects a pattern from a menu — every visible structure is emergent from field math and seed, a master-level implementation where craftsmanship shows in what was left out as much as what remains.
